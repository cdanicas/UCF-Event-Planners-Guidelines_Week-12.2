Event Marketing

Promote your event effectively while following UCF brand standards

Marketing Your UCF Event

Effective marketing is essential for event success. Whether promoting to students, faculty, staff, or external audiences, following UCF brand guidelines ensures professional, consistent communication that reflects well on the university.

UCF Brand Standards

🎨

UCF Brand Guidelines

All UCF event marketing materials must follow official brand standards including proper logo usage, color schemes, typography, and messaging guidelines.

View Brand Standards

UCF Brand Essentials

Official Colors

UCF Black

Hex: #000000

RGB: 0, 0, 0

UCF Gold

Hex: #FFC904 or #FFCC00

RGB: 255, 202, 6

Typography

Primary Font: Gotham

Used for all official UCF communications. Available weights: Book, Medium, Bold, Black.

For digital use, web-safe alternatives may be used when Gotham is not available.

Logo Usage

The UCF logo must be used correctly and never altered. Download official logo files from the brand standards website.

  • Maintain proper clear space around logo
  • Never stretch, rotate, or modify the logo
  • Use approved color variations only
  • Ensure minimum size requirements

Marketing Channels for UCF Events

Social Media

Leverage social platforms to reach your target audience with engaging content.

  • Facebook: Event pages, organic posts, targeted ads
  • Instagram: Stories, posts, reels with event visuals
  • Twitter/X: Quick updates, hashtags, engagement
  • LinkedIn: Professional events, alumni outreach

Tip: Use UCF's official hashtags and tag @UCF when appropriate

UCF Events Calendar

Submit your event to the official UCF events calendar for campus-wide visibility.

  • Reaches entire UCF community
  • Free and official university platform
  • Appears on events.ucf.edu
  • Can be shared across departments

Submit: events.ucf.edu

Email Marketing

Direct communication to your target audience through email campaigns.

  • Departmental email lists
  • Student organization rosters
  • Faculty/staff distributions
  • Alumni networks

Best Practice: Send initial announcement, reminder 1 week before, and day-of reminder

Printed Materials

Physical marketing materials for high-traffic campus areas.

  • Posters and flyers
  • Table tents
  • Banners
  • Digital displays

Note: Follow campus posting policies and obtain approvals for banner placement

Student Organization Networks

Partner with relevant student organizations to amplify your message.

  • Cross-promotion opportunities
  • Access to engaged student communities
  • Shared social media presence
  • Word-of-mouth marketing

Digital Signage

Utilize campus digital displays for visual event promotion.

  • High-traffic building displays
  • Student Union screens
  • Academic building signage
  • Recreation center displays

Note: Contact building coordinators for digital signage submission processes

Marketing Best Practices

Know Your Audience

Tailor your message and channels to your target audience. Students, faculty, staff, and alumni consume information differently and through different platforms.

Create Compelling Content

Use high-quality images, clear messaging, and engaging copy. Highlight what makes your event unique and valuable to attendees.

Be Consistent

Maintain consistent branding, messaging, and visual identity across all marketing channels. Use UCF brand guidelines for all materials.

Start Early

Begin promotion 6-8 weeks before major events. Build awareness gradually and maintain momentum with regular updates.

Use Multiple Channels

Don't rely on a single marketing channel. Combine digital, social, email, and print strategies for maximum reach and frequency.

Make It Easy to Attend

Include all essential information: date, time, location, parking, cost, registration link. Remove barriers to attendance.

Engage and Interact

Respond to comments and questions promptly. Build excitement through engagement, not just announcements.

Track and Measure

Monitor registration numbers, social media engagement, and email open rates. Adjust your strategy based on what's working.

  • ☐ Target audience
  • ☐ Benefits of attending

Social Media Best Practices

Content Ideas

  • Behind-the-scenes event planning
  • Speaker/performer spotlights
  • Countdown posts ("3 days until...")
  • Event sneak peeks
  • Student/attendee testimonials
  • FAQs and quick facts
  • Interactive polls and questions
  • Throwback to past events

Engagement Strategies

  • Ask questions to prompt comments
  • Run contests or giveaways
  • Share user-generated content
  • Respond to all comments and messages
  • Use relevant UCF and event hashtags
  • Tag partners and sponsors
  • Post at optimal times (lunchtime, evenings)
  • Use Instagram/Facebook Stories for urgency

Visual Content Tips

  • Use high-quality, bright images
  • Include people when possible
  • Maintain UCF brand colors
  • Create consistent graphics/templates
  • Use readable fonts and sizes
  • Optimize for mobile viewing
  • Include event details in image
  • Create square and vertical versions
