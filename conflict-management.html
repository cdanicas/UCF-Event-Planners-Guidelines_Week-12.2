Conflict Management

Check event calendars and schedules to avoid conflicts with major university activities

Why Check for Conflicts?

Before finalizing your event date, it's crucial to check multiple calendars to ensure your event doesn't conflict with major university activities, athletics events, or important meetings. This helps maximize attendance and resource availability.

Best Practices for Conflict Management

1. Check Early

Review all relevant calendars as soon as you begin planning your event. Popular dates book quickly, especially during fall and spring semesters.

2. Consider Multiple Dates

Have backup dates ready in case your preferred date conflicts with major events or has limited venue availability.

3. Check All Calendars

Don't rely on just one calendar. Cross-reference campus events, athletics, and official meetings to get a complete picture.

4. Account for Athletics

Football game days (especially home games) can impact parking, traffic, and attendance. Plan around these major events.

5. Consider Academic Calendar

Avoid scheduling during finals week, spring break, or the first week of classes. These periods have low attendance and high stress.

6. Communicate Early

Once you identify a potential conflict, communicate with affected parties or consider alternative dates before finalizing plans.

Quick Tips

  • Holidays: Check federal and state holidays that may affect attendance
  • Campus Closures: Verify the university is open on your event date
  • Exam Periods: Avoid midterms and finals weeks for better student participation
  • Move-In Week: First weeks of fall and spring semesters are extremely busy
  • Graduation Periods: Commencement weeks have limited venue and parking availability
