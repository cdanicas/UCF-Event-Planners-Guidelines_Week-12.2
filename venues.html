Venue Selection Guide

Choosing the Right Venue

Consider these factors when selecting your event venue:

Capacity

Ensure the venue can comfortably accommodate your expected number of attendees with appropriate seating arrangements.

Location

Choose a campus location convenient for your target audience. Consider parking, accessibility, and transportation options.

Amenities

Verify the venue has necessary equipment (A/V, WiFi, microphones) or determine if rentals are needed.

Budget

Compare venue rental fees, setup costs, and any additional charges. Some campus venues offer discounts for student organizations.

Availability

Book early to secure your preferred date. Popular venues fill up quickly, especially during peak academic periods.

Setup Requirements

Consider load-in/load-out times, setup configurations, and whether the venue allows decorations or modifications.
