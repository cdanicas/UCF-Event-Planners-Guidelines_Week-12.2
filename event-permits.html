Event Permits

Understand and obtain necessary permits for your UCF event

Why Event Permits Matter

Certain events require permits and safety assessments to ensure compliance with university policies and local regulations. The UCF Special Activity Form (SAFE) is the primary tool for requesting event approval and coordinating necessary services.

UCF Special Activity Form (SAFE)

📋

Submit Your Event Safety Form

The SAFE form is required for events involving potential risks, outdoor activities, large gatherings, or special circumstances. Submit your form well in advance to allow time for review and approval.

Access SAFE Form

When is a SAFE Form Required?

Submit a SAFE form for events that include:

  • Expected attendance of 50 or more participants
  • Use of outdoor spaces or public areas
  • Physical activities or sporting events
  • Special equipment (inflatables, mechanical rides, etc.)
  • Cooking or food preparation on-site
  • Amplified sound or music
  • Open flames, candles, or pyrotechnics
  • Swimming or water activities
  • Off-campus travel or field trips
  • Serving alcohol
  • Overnight events
  • Use of university vehicles

Permit Submission Timeline

6-8 Weeks Before

Large or Complex Events

Submit SAFE forms 6-8 weeks in advance for:

  • Events with 500+ attendees
  • Events requiring road closures
  • Events with alcohol service
  • Events with special equipment or activities
  • Multi-day events
3-4 Weeks Before

Standard Events

Submit SAFE forms 3-4 weeks in advance for:

  • Standard outdoor events
  • Events with 50-500 attendees
  • Events with amplified sound
  • Events requiring standard permits
2 Weeks Before

Simple Events

Minimum submission time for:

  • Small gatherings in controlled spaces
  • Events with minimal risk factors
  • Indoor events with standard setup

Note: Earlier submission is always recommended to ensure approval

Common Event Permits & Requirements

Outdoor Space Use

Required for events in campus outdoor areas, lawns, plazas, or commons.

  • Submit SAFE form
  • Reserve outdoor space through venue coordinator
  • Consider weather contingency plans
  • Ensure cleanup plan is in place

Amplified Sound

Required for events using speakers, microphones, or DJ equipment outdoors.

  • Submit SAFE form noting sound equipment
  • Follow UCF noise ordinance guidelines
  • End amplified sound by designated times
  • Consider impact on nearby classes/buildings

Food Service

Required for events involving food preparation or cooking on campus.

  • Submit SAFE form for on-site cooking
  • Ensure proper food handling procedures
  • Use approved vendors if catering
  • Follow health and safety guidelines

Alcohol Service

Strict requirements for events serving alcoholic beverages.

  • Follow UCF Alcohol Policy
  • Submit SAFE form well in advance
  • Require security presence
  • Check IDs at entry points
  • Provide non-alcoholic alternatives

Special Equipment

Required for inflatables, bounce houses, mechanical rides, or special structures.

  • Submit SAFE form with equipment details
  • Provide vendor insurance certificates
  • Ensure equipment meets safety standards
  • Have trained operators present

Road Closures

Required for events needing temporary closure of campus roads or pathways.

  • Submit SAFE form 6-8 weeks in advance
  • Coordinate with UCF Parking Services
  • Coordinate with UCF Police
  • Plan alternative routes for traffic
  • Provide advance notice to campus

SAFE Form Approval Process

1

Complete SAFE Form

Fill out the online SAFE form with complete event details, including date, location, activities, and potential risk factors.

2

Submit for Review

Submit the form through the online portal. You'll receive confirmation of submission and a tracking number.

3

Risk Management Review

UCF Risk Management reviews your form and may request additional information or modifications to ensure safety compliance.

4

Department Coordination

The form is routed to relevant departments (Police, Parking, Facilities) for coordination of services and approvals.

5

Approval or Feedback

Receive approval notification or feedback requesting changes. Address any concerns promptly to maintain timeline.

6

Final Confirmation

Once all requirements are met, receive final approval. Keep approval documentation for event day reference.

Permit Application Best Practices

Submit Early

Always submit permit requests as early as possible. Complex events may require multiple rounds of review and revision.

Be Thorough

Provide complete and accurate information on all forms. Incomplete submissions cause delays in the approval process.

Communicate Proactively

Respond quickly to requests for additional information. Maintain open communication with Risk Management throughout the process.

Plan for Contingencies

Include backup plans for weather, equipment failure, or other potential issues in your SAFE form submission.

Follow Up

Check the status of your submission regularly. Don't assume approval until you receive official confirmation.

Keep Documentation

Maintain copies of all approved permits and bring them to your event. You may need to provide proof of approval on event day.

Other Permits You May Need

City of Orlando Permits

Some activities may require permits from the City of Orlando in addition to UCF approval, especially for events involving public streets or off-campus activities.

Fire Marshal Approval

Events with large crowds, tents, candles, or cooking equipment may require inspection and approval from the Fire Marshal.

Vendor Licenses

Outside vendors must provide proof of insurance and appropriate licenses. UCF Risk Management will verify vendor credentials.

Music Licensing

Public performance of copyrighted music may require ASCAP, BMI, or SESAC licensing. Check with your department about existing licenses.

Event Permits Checklist

  • ☐ Determine if your event requires a SAFE form
  • ☐ Review all event activities and potential risk factors
  • ☐ Gather all required information for SAFE form
  • ☐ Submit SAFE form according to timeline (2-8 weeks in advance)
  • ☐ Coordinate with venue for space reservations
  • ☐ Obtain vendor insurance certificates if using outside vendors
  • ☐ Respond promptly to Risk Management questions
  • ☐ Request security if required for your event type
  • ☐ Ensure compliance with all UCF policies
  • ☐ Obtain final approval before promoting event
  • ☐ Keep copies of all approved permits
  • ☐ Bring permit documentation to event
  • ☐ Follow all conditions specified in approval
  • ☐ Complete any required post-event documentation
