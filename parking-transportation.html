Event Parking Best Practices

Contact Parking Services Early

Reach out to UCF Parking Services at least 2-3 weeks before your event to discuss parking needs, permits, and any special arrangements.

Estimate Parking Demand

Calculate expected vehicles based on attendance. Not all attendees will drive, but plan for 60-70% of attendees to need parking for most events.

Identify Nearby Parking

Use the parking map to identify all parking options within walking distance of your venue. Have backup lots identified if primary lots fill up.

Consider Accessibility

Ensure accessible parking is available close to your venue for attendees with disabilities. Reserve accessible spaces if needed.

Provide Clear Directions

Include specific parking instructions in your event communications. Specify lot names, garage levels, and walking directions to the venue.

Plan for Peak Times

Consider parking congestion during class times, game days, or other campus events. Encourage early arrival if parking may be limited.

Transportation Options for Events

Campus Shuttle Services

UCF operates regular shuttle routes across campus. Review shuttle schedules and consider how attendees can use existing routes to reach your event.

  • Review current shuttle routes and times
  • Consider event timing with shuttle schedules
  • Inform attendees about nearby shuttle stops

Chartered Transportation

For large events or off-campus attendees, consider chartering buses or shuttles to transport groups from specific locations.

  • Coordinate with transportation providers
  • Plan pickup/drop-off locations
  • Schedule continuous shuttle loops for large events

Rideshare & Drop-Off Zones

Designate specific areas for rideshare pickup and drop-off to manage traffic flow and ensure attendee safety.

  • Identify safe drop-off zones near your venue
  • Include rideshare information in event details
  • Consider traffic flow and pedestrian safety

Bicycle & Alternative Transport

Encourage sustainable transportation options and ensure bicycle parking is available near your event venue.

  • Identify bike rack locations
  • Promote bike-friendly event access
  • Consider scooter parking areas

Special Considerations for Large Events

Events with 300+ Attendees

  • Work closely with Parking Services to reserve multiple parking areas
  • Consider hiring parking attendants to direct traffic
  • Set up clear signage directing attendees to available parking
  • Plan for traffic congestion and extended arrival times
  • Have staff available to assist with parking questions

Conflicting with Athletics Events

  • Check the athletics calendar early in planning
  • Expect significantly reduced parking on game days
  • Consider alternative venues or dates if possible
  • Strongly encourage carpooling and alternative transportation
  • Extend event start time to account for parking challenges

Evening & Weekend Events

  • Parking is generally more available during these times
  • Verify which lots remain open after business hours
  • Consider lighting and safety in parking areas
  • Coordinate with UCF Police for evening event security
