Group Booking Best Practices

Book Early

For large events, contact hotels 3-6 months in advance to secure room blocks at group rates. Popular dates book quickly, especially during conference seasons.

Negotiate Room Blocks

Hotels often provide discounted rates for blocking 10+ rooms. Negotiate rates, cutoff dates, and any complimentary rooms for event organizers.

Provide Clear Information

Share hotel information in your event communications early. Include booking links, group codes, rate details, and deadlines for group rates.

Consider Transportation

If your event hotel is not within walking distance, arrange shuttle service or provide clear directions and parking information for attendees.

Account for Special Needs

Ensure recommended hotels have accessible rooms, dietary accommodation options, and amenities for guests with special requirements.

Follow Up

Monitor room block pickup rates. Release unused rooms before the cutoff date to avoid attrition fees while ensuring adequate accommodations.

Lodging Planning Checklist

  • ☐ Estimate number of out-of-town attendees needing lodging
  • ☐ Determine appropriate hotel locations based on event venue
  • ☐ Identify hotels with adequate capacity
  • ☐ Research hotel amenities and price ranges
  • ☐ Contact hotels 3-6 months in advance for group rates
  • ☐ Negotiate room block sizes and rates
  • ☐ Establish booking cutoff dates
  • ☐ Request complimentary rooms if applicable (1 free per 50 booked)
  • ☐ Verify accessible room availability
  • ☐ Confirm hotel cancellation policies
  • ☐ Arrange transportation if hotel is not walkable
  • ☐ Include hotel information in event communications
  • ☐ Provide group booking codes to attendees
  • ☐ Monitor room block pickup
  • ☐ Adjust room block if needed before cutoff
  • ☐ Send hotel reminders as event approaches

Tips for Event Attendees

Booking Your Hotel

  • Book Early: Group rates often have limited availability and cutoff dates
  • Use Group Codes: Always reference the event name and group code when booking
  • Confirm Details: Verify check-in/check-out dates, number of guests, and room type
  • Check Amenities: Review what's included (parking, breakfast, WiFi, etc.)
  • Review Cancellation Policy: Understand cancellation deadlines and potential fees
  • Request Accommodations: Notify the hotel of any accessibility or special needs
  • Save Confirmation: Keep your confirmation number and hotel contact information
  • Plan Transportation: Determine how you'll get from hotel to event venue
