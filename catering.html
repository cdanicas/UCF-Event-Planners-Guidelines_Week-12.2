Catering Best Practices

Order in Advance

Place catering orders at least 2 weeks in advance for small events and 4-6 weeks for large events or complex menus. This ensures availability and proper planning.

Accurate Guest Count

Provide the most accurate guest count possible. Plan for 5-10% overage to ensure adequate food. Confirm final numbers 3-5 days before the event.

Dietary Restrictions

Collect information about dietary restrictions and allergies in advance. Provide vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options when possible.

Consider Event Timing

Match your menu to the event time and duration. Morning events need breakfast items, while afternoon meetings may only require snacks and beverages.

Budget Appropriately

Allocate 20-35% of your event budget for food and beverages. Consider cost per person and total attendees when selecting menu items.

Setup and Cleanup

Coordinate with catering staff on setup time, service style (buffet vs. plated), and cleanup procedures. Ensure venue is accessible for delivery.

Dietary Accommodations

UCF Dining Services can accommodate various dietary needs and preferences. When planning your menu, consider these common dietary restrictions:

Vegetarian

Meals without meat, poultry, or fish. Include plenty of vegetable-based options.

Vegan

No animal products including dairy, eggs, or honey. Request plant-based alternatives.

Gluten-Free

Free from wheat, barley, rye, and gluten-containing grains. Label items clearly.

Kosher/Halal

Following religious dietary laws. Discuss requirements with catering coordinator.

Allergies

Common allergens include nuts, dairy, shellfish, soy. Always label potential allergens.

Other Restrictions

Lactose-free, low-sodium, diabetic-friendly options available upon request.

Important Note

Always communicate dietary restrictions to your catering provider in advance. Consider creating separate labeled sections for special dietary items at your event to prevent cross-contamination.

Catering Planning Checklist

  • ☐ Determine expected number of guests
  • ☐ Set catering budget
  • ☐ Identify event meal type (breakfast, lunch, dinner, reception)
  • ☐ Collect dietary restriction information from attendees
  • ☐ Review UCF Dining Services catering menus
  • ☐ Select menu items appropriate for event type and timing
  • ☐ Include vegetarian/vegan options
  • ☐ Plan for dietary restrictions and allergies
  • ☐ Calculate total costs with 10% overage
  • ☐ Submit catering order 2-6 weeks in advance
  • ☐ Confirm venue allows outside catering (if using non-UCF vendor)
  • ☐ Arrange for tables, linens, and serving equipment
  • ☐ Coordinate delivery time and location
  • ☐ Confirm setup and service style
  • ☐ Provide final guest count 3-5 days before event
  • ☐ Arrange for cleanup and disposal
